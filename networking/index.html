How do supervisors and their PhD students experience networking in academia?

Conferences are where academic networks are made — and we want to understand how both sides of the supervisory relationship experience that. Two short online surveys, one for each of you.

How it works

  1. 1Pick your role and complete the survey — 4 to 7 minutes, on your phone is fine.
  2. 2Receive an anonymous code on the last page, together with the matching link for your counterpart.

Choose your role — and start

PhD students

≈ 7 min

Answer first and you receive an anonymous code at the end to forward to one supervisor.

Start the PhD survey→ survey.uni-koeln.de/networking_PhDI
one anonymous code links you

Supervisors

≈ 4 min

Professors, junior professors, postdocs — anyone supervising PhD students. You receive a code to forward to your PhD student(s).

Start the supervisor survey→ survey.uni-koeln.de/networking

Whoever starts first gets the code to forward. The order of participation does not matter — and every single response counts.

Data protection

Participation is voluntary and can be stopped at any time. Responses are collected pseudonymously: the anonymous code is used only to match the two questionnaires of a pair, and is not linked to names, e-mail addresses or institutions. Data are processed in line with the GDPR.
